How to Hash & Notarize

1

Select Your Asset

Choose the correct asset category from the sidebar (Documents, Pictures, Audio, or Videos) to organize your blockchain proofs.

2

Local Cryptographic Hashing

Drag and drop your file. Your browser uses the Web Crypto API to generate a highly secure SHA-256 hash locally. Your actual file never leaves your device.

3

Cheese Blockchain Stamp

Only the unique 64-character hash footprint is transmitted to the Cheese Blockchain via a smart contract, securing your timestamped proof.

4

Immutable Verification

You receive a Transaction ID. Anyone can verify the asset's authenticity in the future by recalculating its hash and searching it in the top bar.
